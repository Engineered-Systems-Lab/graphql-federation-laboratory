Bootstrap: pooled proportions of resampled whole tasks, equal model weights, no refit, no round or session adjustment: an unadjusted cross-check, not a bootstrap of the fitted GEE.

Bootstrap and separation: every resample of whole tasks repeats the observed ceiling or floor of a cell that is all right or all wrong in the data (Guided Direct Access / GPT-6.1 Sol, GraphQL Adapters / GPT-6.1 Sol, Apollo Federation / GPT-6.1 Sol, Cosmo Federation / GPT-6.1 Sol): such a cell adds no width to an interval, and the intervals hold no uncertainty from outcomes absent from the data.

Bayesian mixed model (decision S4): BinomialBayesMixedGLM, mean-field variational Bayes (fit_vb, BFGS, statsmodels defaults), priors vcp_p=1 and fe_p=2, seed 20261028, 4000 posterior draws; posterior mean random-effect standard deviations, task 0.45 and block 0.47 (logit scale). posterior 2.5 and 97.5 percent quantiles of the variational posterior, variance components included.

Bayesian mixed model and separation: the prior shapes the estimates of a separated cell: the data of Guided Direct Access / GPT-6.1 Sol, GraphQL Adapters / GPT-6.1 Sol, Apollo Federation / GPT-6.1 Sol, Cosmo Federation / GPT-6.1 Sol are all right or all wrong, so their likelihood has no finite maximum and their estimates, and every comparison that uses them, are set by the fixed-effect prior (fe_p=2) as much as by the data.
