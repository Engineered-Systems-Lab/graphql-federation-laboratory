## Meaningful transition

Question 6 no longer answers with one count and a share of all missions. It answers with three counts, the missions confirmed with a senior officer, confirmed without and unconfirmed, and one percentage over the confirmed missions only (with divided by with plus without, one decimal, half up, not a number when no mission is confirmed). The people checked became the mission's Starfleet personnel, the commanding officer plus the assigned crew; the participants never count. The unconfirmed missions are shown beside the percentage and are never folded into either side. The commit is on the owned branch `tx/federation-question-6-buckets`, parent lab commit `6b980b1fdcba4e9392aa9d1b4a0d9f2a38ad73a2`, tree `888e3e1`, 11 paths, +818/-99. Exact subject: **Make federation question 6 answer in three counts and one percentage over the commanding officer and the assigned crew**. At the time of this record the commit is not integrated into main.

## Choices recorded

- **The rules are the lead developer's.** The three counts and the percentage over the confirmed missions are their rulings of 2026-10-17, with half up for the rounding. On 2026-10-18 they replaced their earlier ruling not to count the commanding officer for this question: the commanding officer is assigned to the mission, though not in `assignedCrewIds`. The commander is a separate field and is never also in the crew list (0 of the 27 missions that have one). For a mission with nobody at all (no commanding officer and no assigned crew) they chose D: unconfirmed while PLANNED, confirmed without once it has started or ended. They did not decide CANCELLED; the rule treats it like any status other than PLANNED, and no empty CANCELLED mission exists in release 1.0.
- **Expected outcomes first.** `derive_expected.py` and `expected-outcomes.json` were written from the released files before the suite changed. Release 1.1 with every service up: 28 confirmed with, 1 confirmed without, 2 unconfirmed (ST-00000LD4V and ST-00000LD57, both PLANNED), 96.6 percent. Release 1.0 and the Personnel adapter down: 0, 0 and 31, not a number. The file also shows the other option for a mission with nobody (A: confirmed without), which gives 28, 3, 0 and 90.3 percent, and 0, 2, 29 and 0.0 percent. The rule is one constant in the derivation and `emptyMissionRule` in the expected file, read by the suite.
- **A finding on the way.** Before the commander was added, the same rule gave 25, 6, 0 and 80.6 percent for release 1.1, and 0, 4 and 27 for release 1.0 and Personnel down, not the 0, 0 and 31 first written down: four missions had no assigned crew and counted as confirmed without. The commander made two of them non-empty.
- **One graph change.** `Mission.commandingOfficer(release: String!)` is a nullable `PersonnelReference` stub beside `crew(release)`, null when the mission has no commanding officer, with no call made; the plain `commandingOfficerId` is unchanged. The mapping, the Personnel adapter and their outcomes serve it as they serve the crew. The committed supergraph gained the field (7 lines), regenerated by the stack script.
- **Controlled cases.** Sixteen small controlled missions (a commanding officer who is the only senior, a commanding officer who cannot be confirmed, a confirmed senior beside an unconfirmed member, confirmed non-senior members beside an unconfirmed one, one case per kind of unconfirmed member, and missions with nobody at all in two statuses) and five percentage cases (a tie of 6.25 goes up to 6.3, the unconfirmed in neither side, none confirmed) are in the expected file and run through the same client function as the live answer, in two new tests that need no stack. The released-data controlled mission is now ST-00000LD4Q, whose only person is its commanding officer C-85.
- **Tests.** The Apollo stack gate now runs 32 tests, 16 in Part 1 (it ran 30; `test_14` and `test_15` are new) and the three question 6 tests were rewritten. The Missions adapter has one new JUnit test (29 to 30).
